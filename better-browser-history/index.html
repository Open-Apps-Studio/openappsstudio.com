Better Browser History

Find anything you browsed, reconnect your browsing sessions, and understand where your measured foreground time went — without sending your history to a server.

Explore Better Browser History View source

What it gives you

  • Full-text search across saved titles, URLs, and visible page text.
  • Sessions, navigation journeys, repeat visits, stars, tags, and notes.
  • Active-time analytics by website and category, daily trends, and an hourly heatmap.
  • Configurable exclusions, retention, capture controls, and JSON backup.
Your browser stays yours.It replaces only the browser History page. New Tab, homepage, startup pages, bookmarks, and search stay untouched. Capture is off until you enable it, and saved data stays in the extension's local database.
