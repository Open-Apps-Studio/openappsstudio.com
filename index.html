Our principles

Software should be on your side.

The best utility is the one you can understand, trust, and keep using without negotiating with it.

01
Your data stays yours.

We choose local and on-device processing whenever the product allows it.

02
One job, done well.

Focused products beat bloated suites. Every screen has to earn its place.

03
No attention tax.

No ads, manipulative streaks, or artificial urgency—just the tool.

04
Open builds trust.

Our public projects invite inspection, learning, and contribution.

Talk directly to the makers.

The two of us read every message. Questions, thoughtful criticism, and useful ideas are always welcome.
