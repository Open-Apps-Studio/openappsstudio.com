Lingo Lessons

Free language learning for English speakers. Eight courses, no ads, no paywalls, no accounts.

What it does
  • A learn path — sections, units, and lessons that unlock as you go, with XP and a completion flow.
  • Eight courses — Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese.
  • Practice — mistake review and spaced repetition to keep what you learn.
  • Progress — streaks, stats, and per-course progress, all kept on your device.
  • Everything free — no hearts, no energy gates, no sign-up.
