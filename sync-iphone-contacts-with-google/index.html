How to sync iPhone contacts with Google

There are two ways to do it. The built-in iOS setting shows your Google contacts on your iPhone, but only a dedicated app keeps both sides identical, both ways. Here's each, step by step.

Method 1 — Add your Google account in iOS Settings (one-way)

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Contacts → Accounts → Add Account.
  3. Choose Google and sign in.
  4. Toggle Contacts on for that account.

Your Google contacts now appear on the iPhone. The catch: contacts you create in the iPhone's own (iCloud/On My iPhone) account are not copied back to Google, duplicates aren't merged, and edits don't always flow both ways.

Method 2 — Sync Contacts for Google (free, two-way, automatic)

For a single, identical address book on both your iPhone and your Google account, use Sync Contacts for Google. It syncs both ways, automatically, in the background — and it runs entirely on your device.

  1. Install Sync Contacts for Google from the App Store.
  2. Tap Sign in with Google and grant contacts access.
  3. Review the first-sync preview, then tap Sync. Done — it keeps both sides in sync from then on.

FAQ

Does adding a Google account in iOS Settings sync both ways?

Not fully — it shows Google contacts on the iPhone, but iPhone-created contacts aren't copied back to Google and duplicates aren't merged. Use a dedicated app for true two-way sync.

Is there a free way to two-way sync iPhone and Google contacts?

Yes — Sync Contacts for Google is free, with no ads, subscription or contact limits.

Is it private?

Yes. All syncing happens on your device; your contacts and Google sign-in never touch any server.
